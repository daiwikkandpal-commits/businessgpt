Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 July 2026

IGCSE Mark ("we", "us", "the site") provides AI-assisted exam practice and marking for Cambridge IGCSE Business Studies (0450) students. This page explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. IGCSE Mark is an independent study tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Cambridge Assessment International Education.

Information we collect

When you create an account or use the site, we collect:

  • Account information — your name, email address, and school name, provided directly by you or, if you sign in with Google, provided by Google.
  • Marking activity — the questions, mark schemes, and answers you submit for marking, along with the AI-generated feedback and scores returned to you.
  • Usage information — basic technical data such as sign-in timestamps, needed to keep your account secure and working.

We do not collect payment information at this time, as the site does not currently process payments.

How we use your information

  • To create and maintain your account and keep you signed in.
  • To provide AI-generated marking and feedback on the answers you submit.
  • To show you your own progress and marking history over time.
  • To understand, in aggregate, which schools use IGCSE Mark, so we can improve the product.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your data to serve you advertising.

How your answers are processed

When you submit an answer for marking, the text you submit (your answer, plus the relevant question and mark scheme) is sent to a third-party AI provider to generate marking feedback. This is the core function of the site. Submitted answers are used only to generate your feedback and are not used to train third-party AI models beyond standard provider processing.

Third parties we use

We rely on the following service providers to run IGCSE Mark. Each processes a limited set of your data solely to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase — authentication and database storage for your account and marking history.
  • Netlify — website hosting.
  • OpenRouter / Anthropic — AI processing used to generate marking feedback on submitted answers.
  • Google — if you choose to sign in with Google, Google provides us your name and email address for account creation.

Data retention

We retain your account information and marking history for as long as your account remains active, so you can track your progress over time. If you'd like your account and associated data deleted, contact us using the email below and we will action this within a reasonable timeframe.

Your rights

You can request to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time by contacting us. If you're located in a region with specific data protection rights (such as the UK or EU), those rights apply to you in full.

Cookies and local storage

We use browser storage (such as local storage) to keep you signed in between visits and to remember basic preferences like dark mode. We do not use tracking cookies for advertising purposes.

Children's privacy

IGCSE Mark is built for IGCSE-level students, who are commonly minors. We collect only the information necessary to provide the service (name, email, school, and submitted answers) and do not knowingly collect more than this.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time as the site evolves. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision.

Contact us
